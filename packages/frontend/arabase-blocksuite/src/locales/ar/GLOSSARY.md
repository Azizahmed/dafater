# Arabic glossary (editor UI)

One term per concept, shared by `@affine/i18n` (`ar.json`) and these
catalogs. Modern Standard Arabic, short imperative verbs for actions
(«احذف» in descriptions, verbal nouns «حذف» for menu items / buttons).

Keep brand and product names (GitHub, Figma, YouTube, Loom, Markdown, HTML,
PDF, LaTeX, Mermaid) in Latin script. Keep `{placeholders}` unchanged.
"Left" / "right" in the UI mean physical sides (e.g. "Insert Left" =
«إدراج يسارًا»), not start/end.
